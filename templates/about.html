Understanding DASS-21

The Depression, Anxiety, and Stress Scale (DASS-21) is a scientifically validated psychological assessment tool that helps identify and measure emotional states.

Comprehensive Assessment

DASS-21 evaluates three key emotional states: depression, anxiety, and stress, providing a complete picture of mental well-being.

Scientific Validation

Extensively researched and validated across different populations, making it a reliable tool for mental health assessment.

Quick & Efficient

Takes only 5-10 minutes to complete, making it practical for regular monitoring of mental well-being.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I take the DASS-21 assessment?
It's recommended to take the assessment every 2-4 weeks to track changes in your mental well-being. However, you can take it more frequently if you're going through a particularly stressful period.
What should I do if I score high on any scale?
If you score in the moderate to severe range, it's recommended to seek support from campus counseling services or mental health professionals. The assessment results can be a helpful starting point for discussing your concerns.
Is the DASS-21 assessment confidential?
Yes, all assessment results are kept strictly confidential. Only you can access your detailed results, though aggregated anonymous data may be used for research purposes.
How can I track my progress over time?
The system automatically tracks your assessment history and provides visual graphs of your progress. You can view trends in your depression, anxiety, and stress levels over time.
What resources are available if I need immediate help?
Campus counseling services are available 24/7 for emergency support. You can also access national crisis hotlines and online resources through our emergency resources section.
