What is DASS-21?

The DASS-21 (Depression, Anxiety, and Stress Scale) is a 21-item self-report questionnaire designed to measure the three related negative emotional states of depression, anxiety, and tension/stress. It provides a comprehensive assessment of your mental health status.

Stress Categories

Category Description
Normal No significant stress symptoms
Mild Some stress symptoms present
Moderate Moderate stress symptoms affecting daily life
Severe Severe stress symptoms requiring attention
Extremely Severe Extremely severe stress symptoms requiring immediate attention
